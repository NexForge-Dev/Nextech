## How it works

We take part in affiliate programmes run by partner stores and networks. When you click a {{store_name}} link and buy something at the partner store, that store may pay us a commission.

- The commission **does not increase the price** you pay.
- The commission **does not decide** which products appear or the order they appear in search.
- A click **is not** a purchase: we only record sales and commissions once they are confirmed by the partner network or store.

## What this means for you

You buy on the partner store's website, under its terms, warranties and return policies. {{store_name}} is not part of the transaction, never receives your payment details and is not responsible for delivery.
