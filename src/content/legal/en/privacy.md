## Who we are

## What data we process

**Visitors without an account**

- Anonymous usage counts (product views, searches and clicks on “View product in store”), **without** IP address, device identifier or tracking cookie. We use these numbers to learn which products people find most interesting.
- Search terms, not linked to you.
- To limit abuse (rate limiting), we temporarily use a code derived from the IP address, which expires within minutes and is never linked to the events above.

**With your consent to analytics cookies**

- An anonymous session identifier (cookie `nx_sid`) and the page each visit came from within the site.
- Third-party analytics tools, if configured, only load after that consent.

**With an account**

- Name, email and password (stored encrypted by our authentication provider).
- Favorite products and language and country preferences.
- Browsing history, **only if you turn it on** (it is off by default and can be paused and cleared at any time).

**Contact form**: name, email and the message you send.

**We do not process payment data.** Purchases happen at partner stores, which have their own privacy policies.

## Legal bases

Performance of a contract (account and favorites), consent (analytics cookies and browsing history) and legitimate interest (anonymous statistics, security and abuse prevention).

## Cookies

| Cookie | Purpose | Type |
|---|---|---|
| `nx_consent` | Stores your cookie choices | Essential |
| `nx_market` | Stores your chosen country to show the right offers | Essential |
| `NEXT_LOCALE` | Stores the language | Essential |
| `sb-*` | Keeps you signed in | Essential |
| `nx_sid` | Anonymous session identifier for analytics | Analytics (with consent) |

You can change your choices at any time via “Cookie preferences” in the footer.

## Sharing

We use infrastructure providers (hosting and database) that process data on our behalf. When you click an offer, you go to the partner store's website, where its privacy policy applies. Some affiliate networks receive a random click code, with no personal data, to confirm sales.

## Retention

Detailed usage events are kept for up to 13 months and then aggregated into anonymous daily totals. Account data is kept while the account exists. When you delete your account, we delete your profile, favorites and history.

## Your rights

You can access, correct, export (portability) and delete your data, withdraw consent and object to processing. In My account → Privacy you can export your data and delete your account. For other requests, write to {{contact_email}}. You may also complain to your data protection authority.
