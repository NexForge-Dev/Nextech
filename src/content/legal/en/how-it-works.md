Shopping through {{store_name}} is simple:

1. **{{store_name}} presents products and opportunities.** You browse the catalogue, search and compare offers from partner stores.
2. **You choose the product you want.** The product page shows the description, technical specifications and the offers available in your country.
3. **You're sent to the partner store.** When you click “View product in store”, we open the store's website in a new tab.
4. **You pay directly at the partner store.** Final price, shipping, delivery time, returns and warranties follow the terms of the store you buy from.
5. **{{store_name}} may earn a commission on the purchase.** It doesn't change the price you pay.

## Prices and discounts

We show the price reported by the partner store, in its own currency, with the date of the last update. A previous price and discount percentage appear only when they can be proven (for example, an earlier record of the same product at the same store). Some stores don't allow their prices to be shown elsewhere; in that case we show a “See price in store” button.

## Account and favorites

You can browse and save favorites without an account. With an account, your favorites are available on any device, and you can choose to turn on browsing history.
