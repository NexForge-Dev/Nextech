## Does {{store_name}} sell the products?

No. {{store_name}} presents products and sends you to partner stores. Purchase, payment and delivery happen at the store you choose.

## Are prices up to date?

We show the price reported by the partner store and when it was last updated. Prices can change at any time on the store's website; the price that applies is the one shown at the store when you buy.

## Why do some products say “See price in store”?

Some stores and affiliate programmes don't allow their prices to be shown outside their own website. In those cases, you can check the current price directly at the store.

## How do I know a discount is real?

We only show a previous price and discount percentage when we can prove the previous price. We never create fake discounts.

## Do I need an account?

No. You can browse and save favorites without one. With an account, your favorites are saved across devices.

## I had a problem with my order. What should I do?

Orders, payments, delivery, returns and warranties are handled by the store where you made the purchase. Please contact that store directly.

## Does {{store_name}} make money from this?

Yes, partner stores may pay us a commission when you buy through our links, at no extra cost to you. Learn more in “How we earn money”.

## How do I delete my account and data?

Go to My account → Privacy to export your data or delete your account at any time.
