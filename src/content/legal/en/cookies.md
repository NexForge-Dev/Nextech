Cookies are small files stored in your browser. {{store_name}} uses as few as possible.

## Essential cookies (always on)

| Cookie | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| `nx_consent` | Stores your cookie choices | 6 months |
| `nx_market` | Stores your chosen country | 1 year |
| `NEXT_LOCALE` | Stores the language | Session |
| `sb-*` | Keeps you signed in | While signed in |

We also use your browser's local storage to keep favorites for visitors without an account (`nx_favorites`). This data never leaves your device until you sign in.

## Analytics cookies (with consent)

| Cookie | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| `nx_sid` | Anonymous session identifier to understand navigation | 30 minutes |

If third-party analytics tools are configured, they only load after your consent.

## Marketing cookies

We don't use marketing cookies at the moment.

## Changing your choices

Use the “Cookie preferences” link in the footer at any time. You can also delete cookies in your browser settings.
